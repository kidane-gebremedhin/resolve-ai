# Refund Policy

## The 30-day window

Any plan can be refunded in full within 30 days of the initial purchase. After
30 days, monthly plans are not refundable, and annual plans are refunded pro
rata for whole unused months.

## How to request a refund

Email billing@northwind.example from the address on the account. Refunds are
approved within two business days and the money reaches the card in five to ten
business days depending on the bank.

## What is not refundable

One-off professional services, migration assistance, and custom onboarding are
not refundable once the work has started.

## EU and UK customers

Customers in the EU and UK have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal that runs
alongside our 30-day policy. Where the two differ, whichever is more generous to
the customer applies. This means an EU customer on day 20 is covered by our
30-day policy, not restricted to the statutory 14 days.
