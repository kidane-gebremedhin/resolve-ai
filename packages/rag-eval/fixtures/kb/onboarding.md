# Getting Started

## Creating a workspace

Sign up with a work email address. Personal email domains are accepted on
Starter but not on Team or Scale, which require a verified company domain.

## Inviting your team

Owners and admins can invite members from Settings, Team. An invitation expires
after seven days and can be resent.

## Importing data

Data can be imported from CSV or from a supported integration. A CSV import is
limited to 50,000 rows per file; larger datasets should be split.

## Support hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm UK time. Scale customers get
a four-hour response-time commitment during those hours. There is no weekend
support on any plan.
